Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Click the Post Timestamp

Hover over the post's creation time (e.g. "2 hrs ago", "September 1 at 10:30 AM", or "Yesterday") and click it or right-click → "Copy link address".

Clicking the timestamp opens the direct post permalink page.
2

Copy Address Bar URL

Copy the full link from your browser's URL address bar. It will start with facebook.com/.../posts/... or facebook.com/permalink.php?....

https://www.facebook.com/page/posts/pfbid0...
3

For 200+ Photo Albums

If the post is a large album with +33 or +205 photos, click directly on the first thumbnail or the Album Title above the photos, then copy that URL.

https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=...&set=pcb...
1

Tap the "Share" Button

At the bottom right of the Facebook post or album, tap the Share button (arrow icon).

Do not share to your own feed, just open the share sheet.
2

Tap "Copy Link"

From the popup menu, tap "Copy Link" (or swipe down to "More options..." → "Copy"). A link like fb.com/share/p/... is copied.

https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1922bgrBZ3/
3

Paste & Extract

Open FB Downloader PRO on your device, click Paste into the search bar, and click Extract Photos!

Our engine automatically resolves mobile share links to full high-res albums!

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Make sure to avoid these invalid link types:

Avoid

Profile or Page Homepage URL

https://www.facebook.com/username

This only points to the user's overall timeline, not a specific post. Click the exact post timestamp to get the post URL.

Avoid

Notification or Feed URL

https://www.facebook.com/notifications/...

Notification links contain internal tracking redirect IDs. Always navigate to the post itself before copying.

Avoid

Private Group Links without Access

https://www.facebook.com/groups/private_id/...

If a group is set to Secret or Closed, Facebook requires an active login session to render the attached photos.

Why did a post with 30+ photos only show 4 photos on my feed?

Facebook uses a 4-photo collage preview in newsfeeds and overlays a +26 or +205 badge on the last photo. FB Downloader PRO automatically opens the underlying album collection, bypasses the collage truncation, and downloads all hidden photos across the entire album.

Are downloaded photos compressed or uncompressed?

FB Downloader PRO parses the HTML srcset attributes, Comet GraphQL image nodes, and raw network payloads to extract the highest available resolution (e.g. 2048x2048 or original uncompressed dimensions), filtering out all thumbnails and avatars.
